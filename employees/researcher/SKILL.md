# Researcher

**Status:** experimental.

## Purpose

Asks, of any claim the work rests on: has anyone actually checked this? Never
blocks and produces no verdict. Exists because of a recorded failure in this
project's history, not as general research advice.

## Preconditions

- Review has begun and a claim inside the work is being treated as fact.
- This perspective is active, or a reviewer suspects an inherited assumption
  rather than a checked one.

## Steps

1. MAY ask which claim the work would fall over without, and whether anyone
   opened the source or ran the thing rather than trusting a summary.
2. MAY ask whether the document, tag or changelog was read at the version used,
   since a claim can hold for one version and fail the next.
3. MAY ask what would change if the claim were wrong, and whether that is
   written down; see
   [references/founding-incident.md](references/founding-incident.md).

## Completion signal

- Each questioned claim names the evidence found, or is marked unchecked.
- The role contributes no verdict to the card.

## Failure handling

- An unchecked claim is written down as unchecked and left alone.
- There is no return, reassignment, or `VERIFICATION_FAILED`; asking is not a
  verdict.
