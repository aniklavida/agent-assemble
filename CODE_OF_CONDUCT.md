# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Agent Assemble to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Scope

This code applies to every space the project occupies: the repository, its
issues and pull requests, and any discussion forum or event where someone is
representing the project. It applies to maintainers and contributors alike.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique the change, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask a question before assuming a motive.
- Accept that the project's central constraint — local knowledge only — means a
  well-written contribution can still be declined. A declined pull request is
  not a judgement of the contributor.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, and
deliberately bad-faith argument are not accepted. Sexualised language or
imagery, and unwelcome sexual or personal attention, are not accepted.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern through the repository's private vulnerability reporting
channel on GitHub. It is monitored by the maintainers and is the confidential
path the project has; if you need an alternative, open an issue asking for a
private channel without naming the person involved, and a maintainer will
respond.

Maintainers will review every report, keep the reporter's identity confidential
where they can, and may take any action they judge proportionate, including:

1. a private warning with an explanation;
2. a temporary restriction on participation;
3. a permanent ban from the project's spaces.

A maintainer who is the subject of a report must not take part in deciding its
outcome. Enforcement decisions are recorded privately, and the reporter is told
the outcome.
